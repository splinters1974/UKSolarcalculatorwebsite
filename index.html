A genuine return, not just a green gesture

Commercial solar in the UK now stands up without subsidy. Here is why organisations are acting now.

Lock in energy costs

Grid prices have risen sharply and stay volatile. Solar on your own roof gives finance teams more cost certainty.

Meet carbon targets

SECR, supply chain pressure and net zero plans all push down Scope 2 emissions. Rooftop solar is one of the most direct steps you can take.

Mature, proven technology

Commercial panels have been deployed for over two decades. Failure rates are low, warranties long and the UK installer market is competitive.

Predictable returns

Output can be modelled with confidence before a panel goes on the roof, so the business case stands up to scrutiny.

Flexible funding

Buy outright, finance, lease, or sign a Power Purchase Agreement with no upfront cost. Compare the options.

No business rates on it

In England, on-site solar is exempt from business rates until 31 March 2035, and roofs need no extra land.
