Education

Schools, colleges and universities

Solar generation lines up well with the school and college day, and schools typically use 70 to 90% of what they generate. Universities with large estates can host substantial systems across many buildings.

Great British Energy's Solar Partnerships Scheme is funding solar on more than 250 schools, and its Local Power Plan offers further support for public buildings. Live generation data also makes a useful teaching resource.

Agriculture

Farms and rural businesses

Barn and outbuilding roofs are often ideal for solar, and lower-grade land can host ground-mounted arrays while still being grazed. Demand on farms varies a lot, so typical on-site use is 30 to 65%.

Batteries can shift midday solar into the evening for refrigeration, drying and processing, which raises the share you use yourself.
