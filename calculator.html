Questions about the calculator

How do I use the calculator?

Find your site on the map and outline each roof or car park, or type in building dimensions. Add your postcode, the unit price you pay and how much of the solar you expect to use on site. Results update as you go, for buying outright or a Power Purchase Agreement.

How accurate are the results?

They are a well-grounded first estimate, not a design. Generation uses published SAP 10.3 sunshine data for your postcode and a typical layout, so a south-facing roof may do better and a shaded one worse. A site survey and your half-hourly meter data give a firm figure. See how the calculator works.

What does "solar used on site" mean?

It is the share of the electricity your panels generate that your site uses at the time, rather than exporting. Solar you use is worth your full unit price, while exported solar earns around 5p per kWh. The default is 80%.

What is the difference between buying outright and a PPA?

Buying gives the highest return over the system's life because you keep all the savings. With a Power Purchase Agreement a funder pays for, owns and maintains the system, and you buy its electricity at an agreed rate below your grid price. The calculator shows both. See our funding guide.

Does the calculator include battery storage?

Yes, as an option. It sizes a battery from the solar you would otherwise export and shows the extra saving over a 10-year battery life. A battery adds most where a site exports a lot of its solar.

Do the figures include VAT?

No. All costs are before VAT. Commercial installations are charged at 20%, which VAT-registered organisations can usually reclaim.
